Welcome to Newk's Reporting Platform

Custom reporting on top of Toast data, with internal report categories and item mappings.

This platform provides a reporting and dimension layer built on Toast's read-only APIs. Create custom report categories, map menu items to those categories, and generate enriched reports with your own business logic.

Step 1

Toast Menu Sync

Pull the latest menu structure from Toast. This syncs menu items, groups, and modifiers to enable category mapping.

Step 2

Report Categories Management

Create and manage your internal report categories (e.g., "Salads", "Sandwiches", "LTO"). These are Newk's-specific categories separate from Toast.

Step 3

Report Builder (Item Mapping)

Map Toast menu items and modifiers to your internal report categories. Use bulk selection to assign categories to multiple items at once.

Step 4

Net Sales Report

View net sales by business date and location with a pivot table view. Export to CSV for further analysis.
